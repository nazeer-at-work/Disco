## Full Description (max 4,000 chars)
UPGRADE YOUR ANDROID HOME SCREEN WITH DISCO

Turn your phone into a futuristic showcase with Disco: a premium 3D icon pack featuring glossy, disco-inspired app icons crafted with reflective chrome edges, holographic accents, subtle sparkle, and rich 3D depth. 

Every icon keeps your favorite apps instantly recognizable while bathing your home screen in a vibrant, futuristic, and glossy 3D aesthetic that pops on dark, AMOLED, and custom wallpapers.

WHAT YOU GET
• 1,000+ handcrafted glossy 3D icons (with regular updates)
• 15,000+ app activities and launcher mappings for broad coverage
• Reflective chrome edges, holographic highlights, and realistic 3D depth
• True-to-brand color palettes enhanced with glossy reflections
• Built-in Icon Browser with instant search and preview
• Free In-App Icon Request tool: easily suggest missing apps
• Clean high-resolution vector artwork with crisp transparent borders
• Broad system icon support: Samsung One UI, Google Pixel, Nothing OS, OnePlus, Xiaomi & more

HOW TO APPLY
Applying Disco takes just a few seconds:
1. Ensure you have a supported custom launcher installed (e.g., Nova or Lawnchair).
2. Open Disco Icon Pack and tap "Apply".
3. Select your active launcher from the list.
(Or apply directly from your launcher's settings: Settings → Themes / Look & Feel → Icon Style).

REQUEST MISSING ICONS
Can't find a specific app, local banking app, or game? Simply open Disco, navigate to the Request tab, and submit your request. We prioritize community requests in every monthly update!
